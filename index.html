The story behind the board

Made in high school.
A new home
in college.

In high school, I recorded 350 math videos as I worked through the ideas I was learning—from triangle problems and trigonometric identities to calculus and math competitions. I shared them on YouTube, where my videos have received tens of thousands of views.

Some lessons happen at a whiteboard. Others are a pencil, a sheet of paper, and a problem worth exploring. They're personal, hands-on explanations from a student who enjoyed figuring things out and sharing the process.

Now that I'm in college, I've built this website to make that collection easier to learn from. Instead of scrolling through years of uploads, you can browse by subject, narrow down a topic, or search for a particular problem. The whiteboard lessons come first in each subject, followed by the pencil-and-paper recordings.

The videos are the same; finding your next lesson is simpler. Pick a topic, pause when you need to, and try a problem alongside me.

— Benjamin Yazdi
